## Glance Kit 1.17

**Install:** download **GlanceKit.apk** below on your phone and tap it.

### What's new
- **Sunrise, sunset, weather and air quality for where you are.** The app can now use your phone's **approximate location**. It never uses GPS, and it rounds the location to about 1 km. Before, it always used a fixed location.
  - It asks once when you open the app. You can also turn it on or off any time in Setup > Permissions, which shows the location in use.
  - The location only updates while the app is open, never in the background. If you've moved, the widgets and weather refresh straight away.
  - Without the permission, the app uses a built-in default location.
- **Privacy policy updated** to cover location: https://athulsn10.github.io/glance-kit/privacy.html
