## Glance Kit 1.10

**Install:** download **GlanceKit.apk** below on your phone and tap it. It installs over earlier 1.1+ versions.

### What's new
- **Calm scenes for the focus timer.** Pick one under the ring, in the Focus tab or in full screen:
  - **Rain:** streaks at three depths, slanted by a gentle shifting wind, with small splashes and ripples where drops land.
  - **Leaves:** autumn leaves that sway, spin and tumble as they fall.
  - **Snow:** soft flakes at different depths, drifting on a slow breeze.
  - **Fireflies:** warm lights that wander and glow on and off over dark grass.
  - **Stars:** a twinkling sky that turns very slowly, with the odd shooting star.
  - **None:** just the ring.
- The scene keeps going smoothly when the page refreshes. It only animates while it's on screen, and the time stays easy to read on top.

### Also in 1.9
- Drag to reorder the all-in-one widget's pages, with − and + icons to hide or add pages.
- Bug report, contact, feature request and source code links in Setup > About.
