## Glance Kit 1.18

**Install:** download **GlanceKit.apk** below on your phone and tap it.

### What's new
- **Ready for the Play Store.** The app now targets Android 16 (API 36), which Google Play requires for new apps.
- **Play Store bundle.** When the upload key is set up in the repo's secrets, each release also includes **GlanceKit.aab**, signed with your private upload key, ready to upload to the Play Console.
