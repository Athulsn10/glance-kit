## Glance Kit 1.13

**Install:** download **GlanceKit.apk** below on your phone and tap it. It installs over earlier 1.1+ versions.

### What's new
- **Air quality on the Clock widget and Clock page.** A line under the weather shows the US AQI, its level and PM2.5, for example "AQI 72 · Moderate · PM2.5 22". It's coloured by level: green for Good, yellow for Moderate, orange for Unhealthy for sensitive groups, red for Unhealthy, purple for Very unhealthy and dark red for Hazardous.
- The data comes from Open-Meteo (Copernicus CAMS, CC BY 4.0), fetched together with the weather about every 30 minutes. Only the same rough location is sent, no account is needed, and battery use is negligible.

### Also in 1.12
- Weather drawn over the sun or moon on the Clock widget, plus a weather line.
- A Clock page for the all-in-one widget.
