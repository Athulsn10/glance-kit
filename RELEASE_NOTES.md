## Glance Kit 1.14

**Install:** download **GlanceKit.apk** below on your phone and tap it. It installs over earlier 1.1+ versions.

### What's new
- **Soothing sounds for each focus scene.** These are original sounds, synthesised for this app.
  - **Rain:** a steady downpour with gusts, pattering on leaves, the odd plop into a puddle, and **thunder**.
  - **Leaves:** an autumn breeze through the trees, rustling leaves and distant birds.
  - **Snow:** a hushed winter wind with faint, far-off chimes.
  - **Fireflies:** a summer night with crickets and the occasional frog.
  - **Stars:** a slow, calm ambient pad.
- **Thunder and lightning.** In the Rain scene, lightning flashes across the sky with a jagged bolt. Thunder follows a moment later: near strikes crack sooner and louder, and distant ones roll in softly.
- **More realistic rain:**
  - drops with soft motion-blur tails;
  - drifting sheets of heavier rain;
  - gusts that change the slant;
  - crown splashes;
  - beads of water that slide down the "glass" and leave a trail.
- **When the sound plays:** while the Focus screen is open, and through a whole running session even with the screen off. A quiet notification shows while it plays, with a "Sound off" button. It pauses for calls and other audio, and stops when the session ends or is paused.
- **On/off:** a speaker button at the end of the Scene row.

### Also in 1.13
- Air quality (US AQI, PM2.5) on the Clock widget and Clock page.
