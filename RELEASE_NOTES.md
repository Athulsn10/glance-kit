## Glance Kit 1.6

**Install:** download **GlanceKit.apk** below on your phone and tap it. It installs over earlier 1.1+ versions.

### What's new
- **No more flashing:** tapping a button (like Turn on for the live compass) or the minute refresh no longer makes a tab jump to the top and back. Tabs now redraw in place.
- **Memory card** on the Phone tab: live RAM used and free, updated every 2 seconds while you're looking at it, with a 2-minute chart and the apps you used in the last 15 minutes. Android doesn't let one app see another app's memory, so a button opens Developer options > Memory for per-app RAM.
- **Apps card with tabs:** switch between **Screen time** (most used today) and **Storage** (biggest apps, split into app, data and cache). Tap an app to open its storage settings.
- **Cache card:** total cache and the apps holding the most. **Clear cache** opens Android's own dialog to clear every app's cache in shared storage (it asks once for All files access). To clear an app's main cache, tap it and choose Clear cache.

### Permissions
- **Physical activity** for steps (asked once).
- **Usage access** is optional, for full-day data, most-used apps, app storage and cache.
- **Notifications** let the live compass show its "on" notification (asked when you turn it on).
- **All files access** is optional and asked only when you tap Clear cache.
