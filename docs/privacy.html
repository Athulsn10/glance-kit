Glance Kit

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 (updated for the People widget) · App package com.athulsn10.glancekit

In short
  • Glance Kit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. There are no usernames or passwords.
  • Almost everything is read and kept on your phone. It never leaves it.
  • If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location (never GPS), rounded to about 1 km, for sunrise, sunset, weather and air quality.
  • That approximate location is sent to two weather services so the Clock widget can show weather and air quality.
  • People (optional): if you pair with a partner, friend or family, your name, avatar and location are shared only with the people you paired with, through Google Firebase, so their app can show how far apart you are. You can pause or leave at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Who we are

Glance Kit is an open-source Android app of home-screen widgets and a dashboard, published by its developer. You can reach the developer at athulsn34@gmail.com. The source code is public at github.com/Athulsn10/glance-kit.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow approximate location, Android gives the app a coarse position based on Wi-Fi and mobile networks, never GPS. The app rounds it to about 1 km and stores it on your phone. It's only updated while the app is open, never in the background. The app doesn't keep a history of places, and it doesn't track your movements.

If you don't allow location, the app uses a built-in default location instead. You can turn location off at any time in Setup or in Android Settings. Once it's off, the app goes back to the default location.

People: sharing with your partner, friends or family

People is optional and off until you pair with someone. Pairing needs a one-time code made on one phone and typed into the other, so both people have to agree. A group is either two people (Partner or Friend) or up to five (Family).

What's shared, and with whom. When you're in a group, your phone shares these with the people in that group only:

  • the name and avatar you choose (no real name is required)
  • your location, rounded to about 10 m, and when it was taken. Their app uses it to show the distance between you; it never shows a map or your address
  • whether you're "at home" (yes/no, for Family), whether sharing is paused, your time zone, and a mood if you set one for the day
  • the hugs, pokes and other actions you send

How it's sent. This goes through Google Firebase (Firestore database, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), run by Google on our behalf. To use it, the app creates an anonymous ID with Firebase Authentication. There's no email, phone number or password. Firebase stores your phone's push token so actions can reach it. Database rules make sure only people in your group can read your details, and only you can change them. See Firebase privacy and security.

Error reports. If something in People fails (for example, a hug can't be sent), the app saves a short technical report in Firebase so the problem can be fixed: what failed, the error message, the app and Android version, the phone model and your anonymous ID. Reports never include your name, location, avatar or invite codes, and they're deleted after 14 days.

Location for People. People asks for precise location, so that "together" can work, and for "Allow all the time", so the distance can update in the background. Updates happen only at the interval you choose (every 15, 30 or 60 minutes), one location each time. Each new location replaces the last one. No location history is kept, here or in the cloud.

Your control. You can pause sharing at any time; the others then see you as paused. Leaving a group deletes your details from it straight away. A group is deleted when its last person leaves. Uninstalling the app stops all sharing. To have your anonymous ID and data removed, email us.

Data sent off your phone

The Clock widget and the Clock page of the all-in-one widget show weather and air quality. To get them, the app sends requests to two services:

  • MET Norway (api.met.no) for the weather forecast.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for air quality.

Each request contains only your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km. This is your phone's approximate location if you've allowed it, or otherwise the built-in default. It is never precise or GPS location. Like any internet request, these services can also see your IP address. They receive no other information about you or your phone.

These requests happen about every 30 minutes, and only while a Clock widget or Clock page is on your home screen. If you remove them, the requests stop. The results are stored on your phone so the widget can show them. How these services handle requests is covered by their own terms: MET Norway terms and Open-Meteo terms.

Weather data comes from MET Norway, and air-quality data from Open-Meteo using Copernicus CAMS data. Both are licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Sharing, selling and advertising

Glance Kit does not sell or rent your data, and never uses it for advertising. Apart from the weather services above, and the people you choose to pair with through Firebase, nothing is shared. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries; the only error reports are the People ones described above.

Keeping and deleting data

History such as steps, battery and data-use charts is kept on your phone for a limited time, up to about 40 days depending on the chart. To delete everything at once, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. All network traffic, for weather and for People, uses encrypted HTTPS connections. People data in Firebase is protected by access rules, so only members of a group can read it.

Children

Glance Kit is a general-purpose utility and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
